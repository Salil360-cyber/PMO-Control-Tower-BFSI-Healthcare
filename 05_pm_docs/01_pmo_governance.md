# 1. PMO governance

How the PMO runs the portfolio: what every project reports, when, and how status is judged.

## Reporting calendar

| When | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every Thursday 17:00 | PM sends weekly update: % complete, actual cost to date, RAG, top risks, next milestones | Project managers |
| Friday 10:00 | PMO checks the updates (missing, late, numbers that don't add up) and chases gaps | PMO analyst |
| Friday 15:00 | Portfolio status report to the COO office | PMO analyst |
| Monthly, 1st Tuesday | Steering committee: decisions on Red projects, change requests over Rs 5 lakh, resource clashes | Sponsors, PMO lead |
| At closure | Lessons learned and benefits handover | PM, PMO |

## RAG rules (used by the PMO, not only the PM's opinion)

| RAG | Rule (EVM) | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | SPI and CPI both 0.95 or above | On track |
| Amber | Lower of SPI / CPI between 0.85 and 0.95 | At risk, recovery plan needed |
| Red | Lower of SPI / CPI below 0.85 | Off track, sponsor decision needed |

If the PM's RAG is better than the EVM RAG, the project is flagged as a **watermelon** and discussed at the next steering committee.

## Data-quality checks the PMO runs every week

1. Update received from every running project
2. Actual cost never goes down week to week
3. % complete between 0 and 100% and not lower than last week
4. Every open RAID item has an owner and a future due date
5. Every change request over Rs 5 lakh has a sponsor decision within 15 days

## Change control thresholds

| Change | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to Rs 2 lakh and up to 5 days | Project manager |
| Up to Rs 5 lakh or up to 15 days | Sponsor |
| Over Rs 5 lakh or over 15 days, or any scope change to a regulatory deliverable | Steering committee |

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsor | Owns the business case and funding; decides on escalations |
| Project manager | Delivers scope, schedule and cost; sends weekly update |
| PMO lead | Runs governance, chairs the portfolio review |
| PMO analyst | Collects and checks updates, maintains trackers and dashboards, prepares status and steering packs |
