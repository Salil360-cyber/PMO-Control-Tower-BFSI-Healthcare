# 5. Lessons learned (5 closed projects)

| What happened | Evidence | Lesson / action for future projects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four of five projects finished 7-14 days late | P01, P02, P06 +7 days; P05 +14 days | Add a 10% schedule buffer before go-live; track milestone slip weekly, not only at the end |
| Costs stayed close to budget | -1.4% to +4.8% | Keep monthly cost tracking against EV |
| Agile teams delivered most of what they committed | Say-do 89.3%-97.6% | Use the last 3 sprints' velocity to plan; P02 (89.3%) over-committed in early sprints |
| A descope saved the go-live date | P02, CR-02: DSA portal piece moved after go-live | Offer descoping as an option early in status reports |
| Troubled projects sent fewer updates | 9 of 10 missed updates came from P03, P07, P08 | Treat a missed update as an early warning, not admin |
| Shared people became the bottleneck | 4 people over 40 h, each booked on P03, P07 or P08 | Check the resource heatmap before approving a new project |
| Two PMs reported better RAG than their numbers | P07, P08 | RAG must follow the EVM rule; PM's view goes in the comment |
